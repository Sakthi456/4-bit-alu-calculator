Design Observation

Version 2 uses a shared arithmetic unit to reduce hardware resource utilization. It achieves 35.3% fewer Slice LUTs and fewer F7 multiplexers compared with Version 1. However, its post-route delay is slightly higher. This demonstrates the trade-off between hardware area and timing performance.
